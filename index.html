Sample campaign

How I’d launch an apparel collection.

A concept for a collection of everyday pieces that can be styled for different occasions.

Before launch

One piece, three ways

Tease the collection with a quick styling Reel. Show the same piece at work, at brunch and on a night out. Ask viewers which look they’d wear.

Launch week

Give creators room to style

Brief each creator to share an honest fit note, style the piece for their audience and link to the collection with a tagged URL.

After launch

Review what drove interest

Compare Reel saves, product-page visits and attributed orders. Use the results to choose which styling idea to test next.
